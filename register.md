## Register

[Register for the workshop](https://forms.gle/REPLACE-WITH-FORM-LINK)

The form asks for your name, email, department, your Python and git experience,
whether you will attend in person or online, whether you would like to be
considered for partial salary support, and a confirmation that you will
complete [Getting Started](getting-started.md) before Session 1.

Seats are filled in the order people register. If all 30 seats are taken you
are added to a waitlist and offered a place if someone cancels. We will email
you to confirm your seat, and the online link goes to confirmed registrants
only.

Limited partial salary support is available. Answering yes on the form does not
guarantee it.

## Who this is for

- You are comfortable writing and running Python scripts: variables, loops,
  conditionals, lists and dictionaries.
- No software packaging experience is assumed.
- You bring a Windows, macOS, or Linux laptop you are allowed to install
  software on, with about 5 GB free disk space. We recommend 8 GB of memory.
- You complete [Getting Started](getting-started.md) before Session 1. It takes
  30 to 45 minutes and we cannot troubleshoot installations during the sessions.
